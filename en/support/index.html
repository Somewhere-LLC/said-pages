FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. You can use Said without registering a name or email address.
A voice won't play.
Voices play directly from each podcast's host. Check your connection and try again. If the host has replaced the audio, that voice may not play.
The quote doesn't match what was said.
Quotes come from automatic transcription and can contain errors. The audio is the original.
How do I change the time of the daily question notification, or turn it off?
Tap the gear on Home to open Settings, then change the time or turn notifications off under Notifications. If notifications for Said are turned off in iPhone Settings, allow them in the Settings app.
I'm a rights holder and don't want my show in Said's search.
Email us at the address above with the show's name, its RSS feed URL or Apple Podcasts link, and something that shows you hold the rights (for example, sending from the contact address listed in the RSS feed). Once we confirm, we'll remove the show from search. We'll also delete its transcripts on request.
How do I delete my information?
Deleting the App removes the settings stored on your device. For any other deletion request, email us. See our Privacy Policy for details.
