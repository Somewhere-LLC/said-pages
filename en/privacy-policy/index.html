Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy explains how Somewhere LLC ("we") handles information in the iOS app "Said" (the "App").

1. Information we collect

The App does not ask you to register a name or email address. We collect:

  • What you enter to search, and how you use the App
  • An anonymous identifier for your device
  • Device and connection information

2. How we use it

  • To provide the App's service
  • To analyze how the App is used and improve it
  • To prevent abuse
  • To answer your inquiries

3. Third-party services

We use Firebase (including Google Analytics), provided by Google LLC, to analyze usage and prevent abuse. The information sent and why are listed in External Transmission of User Information. Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy.

We may also use other service providers to process information as needed to run the App.

4. Sharing and advertising

We do not share your information with third parties except as required by law. We do not sell or share your personal information, as those terms are defined under California law. The App shows no ads and does not track you for advertising.

5. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for these purposes, then delete it.

6. Your requests

To access, correct or delete your information, email us at the address below. Because the App has no accounts, we may be able to act on a request only in part.

7. Changes

When we change this policy, we will post it on this page with the effective date.
